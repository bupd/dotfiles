# SMART goals reference

Source material behind `SKILL.md`. Load when a goal resists the five checks, or when a batch of goals is being set in one sitting.

## Verbs that make a goal specific

Use these. Each names work that someone performs.

oversee, coordinate, supervise, manage, plan, support, transition, update, upgrade, develop, create, implement, evaluate, produce, write, process, provide, maintain, reconcile, direct, administer

Do not open a goal with improve, reduce, or increase. Those name the direction a number should move and leave the work undescribed. They are fine later in the sentence, attached to a measured result: "reconcile the monthly reports by the 15th with no increase in reconciliation errors".

## What counts as a measurement

Data types worth using: quality or accuracy rate, amount produced, revenue or cost, productivity rate, satisfaction score, count of artifacts shipped.

Collection methods: automated report, audit or test result, survey, the work product itself, an existing document.

A perfect direct measure is often unavailable. The conversation about what success would look like is still worth having, and a named proxy goes in the goal when no direct measure exists.

For a goal running longer than a couple of weeks, add milestones. Milestones are the intermediate steps that add up to the goal, and they carry their own dates.

## Scoping: how big, how many

Start from the buckets of responsibility, then write one goal per bucket. Aim at end results, not tasks.

A goal should be high level enough to cover a real outcome, specific enough to be measurable. If the list of goals is growing long and every item reads like a task, the goals are scoped too low. Combine several into one outcome statement.

Common shapes: increase something, make something, improve something, reduce something, save something, develop someone.

## Statement template

Fill this in when a goal needs rebuilding from scratch.

- Initial goal, in whatever words it arrived.
- Specific: what gets accomplished, who is involved, what steps, why.
- Measurable: what data shows progress and completion.
- Achievable: are the skills, access, and time there; if not, what has to be obtained first.
- Relevant: why now, and which larger objective it serves.
- Time-bound: the deadline, and whether it survives contact with the calendar.
- Final statement, rewritten from the answers.

Compressed form for a one-line title:

> Our goal is to [quantifiable objective] by [deadline]. [Who] will accomplish it by [steps]. This will [result or benefit].

## Worked example: raw goal to SMART goal

Raw: "grow the number of app users".

Specific: grow monthly users of the mobile app by optimizing the app-store listing and running targeted social campaigns.

Measurable: by 1,000 monthly users, across four platforms.

Achievable: four platforms is too many for the team's current load. Cut to three.

Relevant: mobile users stay longer, so app growth feeds revenue.

Time-bound: within Q1 2026, campaigns live from February.

Final: "Grow monthly users of the mobile app by 1,000 within Q1 2026 by optimizing the app-store listing and running targeted social campaigns on three platforms, starting February 2026. Mobile is the primary conversion point for paid signups."

## Example goals by type

Ongoing duties:

- Reconcile the department financial reports by the 15th of every month with no increase in reconciliation errors.
- Provide support that holds a 90% satisfaction rating on accuracy, timeliness, and courtesy.
- Resolve 90% of complaints without escalation.

Project or cycle-specific:

- Transition to the new case management system by building a training program that has every staff member processing 30 cases a day within three months of the last class.
- Reduce department overtime from 150 hours a month to 50 by the end of the fiscal year with no increase in incident reports.
- By 2026-11-30, update the handbook and publish a searchable intranet version.

Development goals:

- By 2026-06-30, develop the computer skills needed to produce the monthly budget reconciliation on time and without errors.
- Complete the coursework and attain the credential by 2026-06-30, measured by supervisor feedback and the performance plan goals met.
