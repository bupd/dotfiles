## Editor formatting constraints (Draft.js)

X Articles uses a Draft.js rich-text editor, not markdown. Hard rules when preparing content for paste:

1. **Every newline = new paragraph block.** Join all sentences of a paragraph on ONE line; blank lines separate paragraphs.
2. **Plain/rich text, not markdown.** `**bold**` and `##` do not render from paste; formatting comes from HTML clipboard paste or manual editor formatting.
3. **No em dashes.** Replace with colons or rewrite.
4. **`---` dividers are ignored as HTML `<hr>`.** They must be inserted via the editor's Insert > Divider menu.
5. **Tables don't render natively.** Convert to PNG images before publishing.
6. **Code blocks** get converted to blockquotes; keep snippets short or screenshot them.
7. **H1 is the title only** — body headings start at H2.

Supported natively: H2 headings, bold, italic, links, ordered/unordered lists, blockquotes.

## Image specs

| Type | Aspect ratio | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|
| Cover/header | 5:2 | 1792×716 |
| Inline images | 16:9 or 4:3 | 1792×1024 |

First image in the source markdown becomes the cover.

## Embed limitation

Embedded posts ALWAYS render at the END of the content block, never inline. Structure the article to say "see the posts below", and insert embeds via the Insert > Posts menu, not by pasting URLs.

## Viral article template

```
HOOK (hit an insecurity or an opportunity)
WHAT IT IS (1–2 paragraphs with social proof)
WHY MOST PEOPLE WON'T DO IT (address objections early)
THE [X]-MINUTE GUIDE (steps with time estimates)
YOUR FIRST [N] WINS (immediate copy-paste value)
THE COST (value comparison: $20/mo vs $2000 VA)
WHAT TO DO AFTER (next steps)
THE WINDOW (urgency)
CTA (soft or hard)
```

Minimum 800 words; under 500 reads as a padded tweet. Sweet spot 1,000–1,500.

## Hook patterns

- **Insecurity/FOMO**: "everyone's talking about X... and you're wondering if you missed the window"
- **Big opportunity**: "this is the biggest opportunity of our lifetime"
- **News hook**: "X just open sourced the algo. Here's what it means for you:"
- **RIP**: "RIP [profession]. This AI tool will [action] in seconds."
- **WTF**: "WTF!! This AI Agent [does amazing thing]. Here's how:"
- **Personal story**: open with a specific, dated, first-person moment
- **Mystery/symptom** (technical pieces): "Our jobs sat in Queued for minutes. Nothing had changed."

## CTA patterns

- **Hard (engagement bait)**: "RT + follow + reply 'KEYWORD' and I'll send the cheat sheet"
- **Soft**: "If you build something with this, let me know!"
- **Simple**: "A like/RT helps if this was useful."

## Style archetypes

Pick one and stay consistent:

- **Tactical** (Damian Player): all lowercase, urgent, 1500+ words, heavy step-by-step, hard CTA with lead magnet
- **Motivational** (Alex Finn): normal caps, warm, 800–1200 words, mixes WHY and HOW, soft CTA + product links
- **Philosophical** (Dan Koe): 2000+ word essay, personal story opener, named frameworks ("The Pyramid Principle"), newsletter CTA
- **Engineering blog** (default for technical users): first person plural, concrete numbers, story arc, dry humor allowed, sources section

## Promo / posting strategy

- Launch tweet under 280 chars: hook + article link; tag 2–3 relevant people max
- Self-reply with a TLDR immediately after posting
- 4–5 follow-up tweets spaced over 48 hours, each surfacing one standalone insight from the piece
- Prepare 3–4 rebuttals for the most likely pushback before publishing

## Tweetable quote patterns

- **Result + cost**: "I gave an AI agent full access to my MacBook. It checks email, pushes code. $20/month. A VA costs $2000."
- **You don't need X**: "You don't need a server. I'm running this on an old MacBook Air from a drawer."
- **Gap warning**: "The gap between 'has X' and 'doesn't' is about to get massive. Mine took 15 minutes."
- **Urgency**: "Most people will bookmark this and never do it. Don't be most people."

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Hook in the first line
- [ ] Objections handled early
- [ ] Steps have time estimates
- [ ] Immediate-wins section present
- [ ] CTA at the end
- [ ] No em dashes; sentences joined on single lines
- [ ] Cover image 5:2
- [ ] Embeds referenced as "below", inserted via menu
- [ ] Tables converted to PNG
- [ ] Read aloud once for AI-sounding phrasing
