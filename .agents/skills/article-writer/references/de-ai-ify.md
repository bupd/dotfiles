# De-AI-ify: Strip AI-Sounding Patterns

Run this pass over every draft before delivering. Keep meaning; cut the tells. When in doubt, cut rather than rephrase.

## Filler phrases to remove

- "Let's dive into...", "Let's explore..."
- "I'd be happy to..."
- "It's worth noting that..."
- "It's important to understand..."
- "In today's rapidly evolving..."
- "This is a great question"
- "As we can see..."
- "Moving forward..."

## Hedging to reduce

- "It could potentially..." → state directly or remove
- "This might suggest..." → "This suggests..." or cut
- "It's possible that..." → state the thing, qualify once at most
- Stacked qualifiers ("perhaps", "maybe", "somewhat", "arguably") → keep at most one, only where uncertainty is real

## Structural patterns to fix

- Bullet points where prose is clearer (bullets are for genuinely enumerable items)
- Every paragraph opening with a transition word
- Formulaic "In conclusion..." / "To summarise..." wrap-ups
- Numbered lists for things that aren't sequential
- Restating the question before answering it
- Em dashes anywhere (strongest single tell — colons, periods, or rewrite)

## Tone patterns to adjust

- Overly enthusiastic: "fantastic", "incredible", "amazing", "game-changer"
- Corporate-bland: "leverage", "synergise", "drive value", "utilize"
- Sycophantic agreement before making a point

## Rewrite rules

- Keep the author's intent and meaning; preserve technical accuracy, links, and any frontmatter
- Active voice, concrete language, shorter sentences
- Cut throat-clearing; let content speak
- Do not remove content or add new content — only rephrase
- Preserve the author's voice where it comes through

## Standalone use

Also usable as a pass over external text: read the target, flag patterns per the lists above, rewrite flagged sections, show before/after diff, apply only after the user confirms.
